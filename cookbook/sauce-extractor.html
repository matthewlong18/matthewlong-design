Sauce Extractor

Turns an hour of podcast into the few ideas worth keeping, and what to do with them. It's the Claude skill I use most.

Prep time
2 minutes
Roast level
●●● Light (easy)
Serves
Anyone who saves things "to read later"
What you need

Ingredients

  • Claude Any plan works for the copy and paste version.
  • Something long to chew on A podcast or video transcript, an article, a thread, or your own messy notes.
  • Three lines about you Optional. Leave them blank and Claude uses what it already knows about you.
  • Notion Optional. If it's connected, the skill offers to save each extraction there.
How to make it

Method

  1. Copy the recipe. One button, just below.
  2. Paste it into a new Claude chat and send it. Claude now knows the recipe for that chat.
  3. Paste your transcript or article and say extract the sauce.
  4. Liked it? Save it for good. Pasting only lasts for one chat. Add it as a skill and Claude keeps it.

Save it for good: add it as a skill

Pasting works for one chat. Adding it as a skill means Claude reaches for it on its own, in any chat.

  1. Download the skill (.zip). Don't unzip it.
  2. In Claude, open Customize, then Skills, and upload the .zip.
  3. Switch the skill on. Skills need code execution turned on in your Claude settings.

Menus move around. If yours looks different, Anthropic's guide to custom skills has the current steps.

Serving suggestion

What comes out

In

The transcript of an hour-long podcast episode about AI agents.

Out · key insights
  • Chat vs. agent. Chat is question → answer. An agent is goal → result: it keeps going until the job is done.
  • Skills are SOPs for AI. Build the process once, then call it by name instead of explaining it again.
  • MCP is the translator. It sits between Claude and your tools so they can talk to each other.

From one of my real runs, trimmed. The full output also gives you action steps, quotes worth keeping, and tags.

Chef's notes

Two things worth knowing

Paste the transcript, not the link. In most setups Claude can't watch a YouTube video from a link. Use the "Show transcript" option under the video, copy it all, and paste that in.
Season it with you. The more Claude knows about your work, the more useful the action steps get. Three lines is plenty.
